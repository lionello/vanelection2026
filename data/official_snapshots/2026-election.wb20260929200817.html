2026 Vancouver Election

Election day: Saturday, October 17

You will vote on: 

  • 1 mayor
  • 10 City councillors
  • 7 Park Board commissioners
  • 9 Vancouver School Board trustees
  • 3 Capital Plan questions
  • 2 plebiscite questions

Apply to vote by mail

Request your Vote by Mail package by October 5 at noon to have it mailed to you. Deadline to request a ballot for pick up at the Election Office is October 13.

Apply early if you will be out of town.

Completed ballots must be returned by 8pm on October 17.

Plan your visit

West Broadway is temporarily closed to vehicle traffic between Cambie St and Alberta St because of construction. 

  • Accessible reserved parking is available on Alberta St, north of West 8th Ave.
  • Limited reserved parking is available on West 8th Ave, east of Alberta St.
  • Bus routes have been detoured to West 8th Ave between Cambie St and Alberta St.

Phone ahead to plan your visit. Visit translink.ca/alerts External website, opens in new tab for updates on transit detours and temporary bus stop locations.
