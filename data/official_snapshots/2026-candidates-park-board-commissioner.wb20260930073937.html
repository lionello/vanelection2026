Plan Your Vote

Choose candidates, answer Capital Plan borrowing and plebiscite questions, and pick where and when to vote.

The Vancouver Park Board is made up of 7 commissioners. 

The Park Board is responsible for managing City parks and community centres, and provides a wide-range of recreation services.
